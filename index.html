Research Program

Multidisciplinary Chromatography & Mass Spectrometry

At the MCM Lab, our research is driven by a commitment to exploring the frontiers of science and technology. We specialize in the analysis and characterization of complex biological and chemical systems using cutting-edge chromatography and mass spectrometry techniques. By integrating high-resolution separation science with advanced mass spectrometric detection, our group develops innovative analytical workflows across biopharmaceuticals, multi-omics, and environmental/material sciences.
